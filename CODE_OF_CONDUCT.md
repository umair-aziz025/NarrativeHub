# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want NarrativeHub to be a welcoming, respectful project for writers, builders, maintainers, and contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without consent.
- Help keep storytelling spaces safe for the communities that use them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized or discriminatory language in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Attempts to introduce malicious code, hidden tracking, or credential theft.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block users whose behavior harms the project or its community.
